## Cross-Cutting Quality, Automation, DevOps, and Architecture Agents

This domain holds **reusable, cross-domain** roles that provide short, expert reviews and shared engineering practices.

### Roles (agents)

- `qa-architect`: cross-domain test strategy, coverage, risk-based planning.
- `automation-architect`: test frameworks, pipeline patterns, environments for testing.
- `devops-platform-architect`: CI/CD standards, IaC, environments, release engineering patterns.
- `security-architect`: threat modeling, secure design reviews, guardrails.
- `observability-architect`: logs/metrics/traces, SLOs, alerts, operational readiness.

### Typical interactions

- These agents are usually invoked **after** a design exists and before implementation or release.
- They provide **brief reviews** focusing on:
  - risks, missing requirements, and unsafe assumptions
  - concrete mitigations and next steps

### Key skills commonly used

- `architecture-review`, `security-review`, `ci-cd`, `testing`, `log-analysis`, `performance-tuning`
